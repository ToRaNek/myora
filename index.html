The coach that decides for you

Myora is a strength training and nutrition coach for Android. It builds your program, dictates every set, load and rest time, and sets your calories and macros, all from the sports science literature.

What it does

  • Program generated from your split: hypertrophy, strength or toning.
  • Loads prescribed set by set and adjusted from what you actually lift.
  • Calorie and protein targets that adapt to your weigh-ins and food journal.
  • Demonstration for every exercise, rest timer, progress charts.
  • Available in 40 languages.

Your data stays on your phone

No account, no server. Your profile, sessions and food journal are stored on your device only.
