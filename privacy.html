Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Your training and nutrition data

Everything you enter in Myora (profile, body weight, body-fat percentage, training sessions, food journal) is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to any server: we collect and store no personal data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) and its advertising partners. To deliver and measure ads, these partners may collect the device advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interaction data. This data goes directly to them, not to us. Where required by law (EEA, UK, Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before any personalised advertising. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings. See Unity's privacy policy.

Subscription

The optional "no ads" subscription is processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details; the app only learns whether the subscription is active.

Children

Myora is not directed to children under 16.
